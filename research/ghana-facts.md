## Seen only in search summaries (not yet read on the page; verify before using)

- Bank of Ghana policy rate held at 14.0% on 24 September 2026.
- About 35% of MSMEs have access to bank financing (Development Bank Ghana / University of Ghana Business School / Bank of Ghana research).
- Mobile money, June 2026: about 954 million transactions, about GH¢493 billion, 84.6 million registered accounts, 26.4 million active.
- Bank one-year SME loan APR up to 33.58% (Bank of Ghana May 2026 data, via Citi Newsroom).

## How the app uses these
- Default interest rate in the app is 35%, the midpoint of the microfinance range above. The survey does not say flat or reducing-balance, so the app labels it an editable assumption.
- The pitch can say: the central bank says mobile money records are credit records, and a reported $4.8 billion annual SME financing gap shows no one is turning them into credit files yet. Proof of Business does that and checks every number.

## What is synthetic
The three traders (Abena, Kofi, Ama) and their statements are invented. They are generated by `backend/app/personas.py`, contain no real people, and no mobile money brand names. Say so in the video and README.
